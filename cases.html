Judicial Information System

Cases & Dockets

Search public United States District Court case records by docket number, caption, party, statute, or offense. Case pages combine the docket, public filings, charges, and recorded dispositions held by JIS.

Search District Court cases

Searches use the public JIS case index. For published Supreme Court decisions, use the Supreme Court case-law search.

JIS case records are derived from official court source records. A case being closed or placed in a completed-case list does not itself establish a conviction.

Charges and convictions are distinct records. A criminal charge is an allegation. A conviction is displayed only when JIS contains a verified convicted disposition for that defendant and count.

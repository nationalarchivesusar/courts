About the judiciary

United States Courts

This is the public information and legal-resource website of the USAR federal judiciary. It provides access to current court dockets, Supreme Court case law, Rules of Procedure, documents and forms, and related federal legal information.

The Federal Judiciary

The USAR federal judiciary consists principally of the Supreme Court of the United States and the United States District Court. It does not include an intermediate Court of Appeals.

Highest court

Supreme Court of the United States

The Supreme Court is the highest court of the United States. It exercises the jurisdiction provided by the USAR Constitution, federal law, and the Rules of the Supreme Court. Appeals from the United States District Court proceed directly to the Supreme Court where permitted.

Federal trial court

United States District Court

The District Court hears federal criminal prosecutions, civil actions, and other matters within its jurisdiction. Its public docket is organized by the judges responsible for active matters.

Current Information Services

Case Search Scope

Case Search uses CourtListener’s public case-law index and is presently scoped to published and other available Supreme Court material. It does not search USAR District Court docket records or arrest records.

Docket Data

Current docket information is synchronized from the public working records maintained by the respective courts.

Maintenance

This website represents the United States Courts. The National Archives and Records Administration maintains and hosts the public website and its associated legal-information infrastructure for the judiciary.

This is an independent USAR community resource. It is not affiliated with the real United States government and does not provide real-world legal advice.

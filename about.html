About the judiciary

United States Courts

This is the public information and legal-resource website of the USAR federal judiciary. It provides access to current proceedings, structured case records, court rules, forms and templates, case law, locally archived opinions, and public judicial records.

The Federal Judiciary

The USAR federal judiciary consists principally of the Supreme Court of the United States and the United States District Court. It does not include an intermediate Court of Appeals.

Highest court

Supreme Court of the United States

The Supreme Court is the highest court of the United States. It exercises the jurisdiction provided by the USAR Constitution, federal law, and the Rules of the Supreme Court. Appeals from the United States District Court proceed directly to the Supreme Court where permitted.

Federal trial court

United States District Court

The District Court hears federal criminal prosecutions, civil actions, and other matters within its jurisdiction. Current proceedings and structured public case records are available through separate docket and case-search services.

Resources Available Here

Cases & Dockets
Current Dockets show active proceedings. Case Search provides structured public JIS case records, and Filed Document Search searches available public filings.
Judges & Records
The Judges directory shows the active District Court bench and current assignments. Arrest & Conviction Records provides the public JIS person-record search.
Court Rules
The Court Rules directory keeps Rules of Procedure and Codes of Conduct distinct while making both collections easy to browse.
Forms & Templates
The Forms & Templates library contains reusable filing resources. It is separate from documents actually filed in court cases.
Case Law & Opinions
Case Law searches the CourtListener Supreme Court index, while the USAR Opinions & Orders archive exposes locally preserved court decisions and order lists.
Federal Law
The related United States Code Library provides searchable federal statutory law.

How Court Information Is Published

Current Dockets

Current Supreme Court and District Court docket displays are synchronized from the courts' public working records and remain separate from the structured JIS case database.

Structured Case Records

Case Search, Filed Document Search, Judges, and public arrest or conviction records use allowlisted public projections from the Judicial Information System. A charge, arrest, filing, and conviction are treated as distinct legal records.

Site Search

The masthead search finds pages, hosted rules and conduct provisions, reusable forms, and locally archived opinion filenames. It does not replace the dedicated JIS case, filing, or person-record search tools.

Maintenance

This website represents the United States Courts. The National Archives and Records Administration maintains and hosts the public website and its associated legal-information infrastructure for the judiciary.

This is an independent USAR community resource. It is not affiliated with the real United States government and does not provide real-world legal advice.

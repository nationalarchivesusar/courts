About this service

United States Courts

This website is the public legal-information portal for the USAR federal judiciary.

Phase I

The site succeeds the former USAR Legal Directory. Its existing federal and Supreme Court rules, rulings, document templates, dockets, and search resources have been retained while the service adopts a court-wide identity.

Maintenance

The judiciary remains the primary identity of this website. The National Archives and Records Administration maintains and hosts the site for the United States Courts.

Scope

This phase does not yet provide a unified court-records system or public judiciary API. Case Search is currently limited to Supreme Court case law indexed by CourtListener, and docket summaries continue to use the established court boards.

Visitor assistance

Help using this site

How do I find a court rule?
Open Rules & Procedure in the primary navigation, choose a rule set, or use the site search to search across all five collections.
What does Case Search include?
The current tool searches Supreme Court of the United States case law in CourtListener. It does not yet search every USAR court or public record.
Where are filing templates?
Visit the Document Library to filter and search the existing forms and templates.
Who maintains this website?
The National Archives and Records Administration maintains the website for the United States Courts.

Report a site problem

Contact the National Archives through the linked USAR community channel in the footer. Include the page address and a short description of the problem.
